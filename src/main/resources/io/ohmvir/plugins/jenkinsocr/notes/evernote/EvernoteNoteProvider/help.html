Stores notes in Evernote. A note's path is mapped onto stacks and notebooks: Biology is the notebook "Biology", and Math/Linear Algebra is the notebook "Linear Algebra" in the stack "Math". Missing notebooks are created, and a note whose title already exists in its notebook is appended to.

Evernote cannot render equations. With the LATEX or KATEX formula output type, formulas are kept as raw TeX source in monospace and builds print a warning; set the formula output type to PURE_TEXT for readable notes.
